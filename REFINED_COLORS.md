# ✅ HOMEPAGE REFINEMENT - EASY ON THE EYES

## 🎨 Color Palette Update

### Old Colors (Too Intense):
❌ Purple: #8B5CF6 (Very bright purple)
❌ Fuchsia: #D946EF (Very bright pink)
❌ Cyan: #06B6D4 (Very bright cyan)
❌ High opacity glows (50-80%)

### New Colors (Subtle & Elegant):
✅ Indigo: #6366F1 (Softer blue-purple)
✅ Slate: #64748B (Neutral gray-blue)
✅ Blue: #3B82F6 (Softer blue)
✅ Low opacity glows (15-25%)

---

## 🌟 What Changed:

### 1. **Background Glows**
- **Before**: Bright purple, fuchsia, cyan with 30-60% opacity
- **After**: Subtle indigo, slate, blue with 15-25% opacity
- **Result**: Much softer, easier on eyes

### 2. **Neuron Particles**
- **Before**: 25 bright purple/blue particles
- **After**: 20 subtle slate/indigo particles
- **Colors**: Slate gray and soft indigo
- **Opacity**: Reduced from 40-80% to 20-50%
- **Size**: Smaller (1px instead of 1.5px)

### 3. **Badge**
- **Before**: Bright purple/fuchsia/cyan gradient border
- **After**: Subtle indigo/slate border
- **Text**: Simple gray instead of gradient
- **Removed**: Pulsing animation and lightning bolt
- **Result**: Clean, professional look

### 4. **Gradient Text (Name)**
- **Before**: Purple → Fuchsia → Cyan (very vibrant)
- **After**: Indigo → Slate-Light → Blue (soft and elegant)
- **Result**: Beautiful but not overwhelming

### 5. **Buttons**
- **View Projects**: Indigo to blue gradient (subtle)
- **Get in Touch**: Glass with slate border
- **Removed**: Intense glow effects and sparkle rotations
- **Result**: Professional, clickable

### 6. **Orbital Center**
- **Before**: Bright purple/fuchsia/cyan with strong glow
- **After**: Slate to indigo with subtle glow
- **Ethereum Icon**: Light gray instead of pure white
- **Size**: Slightly smaller
- **Glow**: 30-50% less intense

### 7. **Orbital Rings**
- **Before**: Purple, cyan, fuchsia borders with strong glow
- **After**: Slate and indigo borders with minimal glow
- **Opacity**: Reduced from 20-30% to 10-20%
- **Shadow**: Much softer

### 8. **Connection Lines**
- **Before**: Purple with 20-50% opacity
- **After**: Slate with 10-30% opacity
- **Result**: Barely visible, just adds depth

### 9. **Tech Icon Cards**
- **Before**: Purple/cyan gradient glow on hover
- **After**: Subtle indigo/slate glow on hover
- **Border**: Slate instead of purple
- **Hover glow**: 50% less intense
- **Removed**: Particle trails

### 10. **Hexagons**
- **Before**: Purple borders
- **After**: Very subtle slate borders (8% opacity)
- **Result**: Almost invisible, just adds texture
